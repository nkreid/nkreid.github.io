Austere Surgical was founded by four engineers and clinicians who met at the Texas A&M EnMed program — a joint MD / Master of Engineering pathway built specifically for physician-engineers. Different specialties, one shared problem: the tools we use to operate haven’t kept up with where care actually needs to happen. Pictured at our EnMed graduation. From left: Drew Hendricks, Jacob Paris, Nick Sears, PhD — one of the engineering faculty members who helped Austere Surgical get off the ground — Brayden Raper, Koby Reid.
