Surgical readiness, anywhere on Earth — or off it.

Austere Surgical builds rugged equipment systems for invasive medical procedures in remote, resource-limited, and extreme environments — from rural emergency rooms to the back of a mobile ICU to the lunar surface.

Our Mission

Care happens where the patient is — not where the operating room used to be. We design tools that let trained providers sterilize, organize, and deploy the equipment they need for invasive procedures in the field, on the move, and outside the four walls of a traditional OR.

What We Build

The RAHKit

Rugged Austere Health Kit. A self-contained system to sterilize, store, and display every instrument and supply needed for an invasive procedure — including a setup for the first assist. Replaces the mayo tray for environments where stability, time, and space are constrained.

Built for field medics, crew medical officers, and anyone whose “OR” is a Pelican case in the back of a vehicle.

The Wild OR

Our broader concept of operating-room-on-demand: integrated workflow, sterilization, lighting, and instrument access designed from scratch for environments where a fixed OR isn’t coming.
